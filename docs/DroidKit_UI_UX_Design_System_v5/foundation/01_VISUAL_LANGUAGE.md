# Visual Language
DroidKit should feel like a professional Android diagnostic instrument—not a generic settings app or cyberpunk game.

## Material 3
Platform interaction, navigation, sheets/dialogs, accessibility and Compose primitives. Customize through DroidKit tokens.

## Minimalism
Technical screens favor readable key/value information and hierarchy over decoration.

## Dark Technical
Dark-first graphite surfaces, crisp text, technical typography for raw values, subtle depth. Complete light theme required.

## Bento
Home and selected overview dashboards only. Avoid in dense inspectors/settings/forms.

## Aurora / Gradient
Candidate-4 green -> teal identity for splash, hero accents, AI identity, onboarding and focus moments. Not every card/button.

## Restrained Glass
Only rare elevated contextual/AI surfaces when contrast and performance remain good. Dense data stays solid.

Not primary: Neumorphism, full Cyberpunk, Brutalism, Claymorphism, Maximalism or full Liquid Glass.

**Decoration decreases as information density increases.**
