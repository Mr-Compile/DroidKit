# 17 — Dialog & Bottom Sheet Behavior

Standardize purpose:
- Dialog: confirmation, destructive/high-impact decisions, concise blocking decisions.
- Bottom sheet: contextual options/details where appropriate.

Rules:
- Clear title and consequence
- Explicit primary/secondary actions
- Risk level reflected in copy/visual semantics
- Back/outside-tap behavior intentional
- Prevent duplicate submissions
- Preserve accessibility focus
- Never hide critical consequences behind vague labels such as OK
